The Story

Hey there, I'm Lorenzo.

I'm a software developer and creator based in Italy. I have a deep passion for building polished digital experiences, exploring distributed systems, and turning complex ideas into clean, usable code.

My journey in programming started from a desire to create and understand how things work under the hood. Over the years, I've worked across the full stack—from designing intuitive UI interfaces and themeable design systems to architecting high-performance backend microservices and CLI tools.

When I'm not writing code or experimenting with new technologies, you can find me reading about systems design, writing essays on productivity and technology, or exploring new cities and architecture.

Philosophy

How I Approach Software

Principle 01

Simplicity Over Cleverness

Clean, readable code with minimal dependencies is always easier to debug, maintain, and scale than over-engineered abstractions.

Principle 02

Obsession with Performance & UX

Speed is a feature. Fast load times, responsive interactions, and thoughtful visual feedback elevate good software into exceptional software.

Principle 03

Continuous Learning & Building

The best way to master new concepts is by shipping real projects, testing assumptions, and sharing the journey openly.
