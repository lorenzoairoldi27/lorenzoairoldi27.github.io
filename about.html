About me

The Story

Curious about spaces, markets, and the future of living.

Based in Milan, I work at the intersection of economic analysis and contemporary real estate. My interest began with a fascination for urban development—how cities evolve, how architecture influences well-being, and how capital flows shape the skylines of modern Europe.

Having studied economics and financial management, I naturally bring a data-oriented mindset to real estate. Rather than viewing property purely through traditional sales channels, I treat it as an asset class where structural quality, bioclimatic efficiency, and location scarcity converge.

Beyond client advisory, I actively research housing trends, write essays on market dynamics, and explore the potential of proptech and smart sustainable architecture.
