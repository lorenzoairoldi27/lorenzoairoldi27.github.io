Consultant Profile

A fresh, data-driven perspective on prime real estate.

Professional Background

Real estate advisory built on intellectual honesty and fiduciary commitment.

I founded my advisory practice with a clear observation: traditional real estate agencies often focus on quick transactional turnover rather than long-term asset alignment.

With a background grounded in quantitative economics and international business, I approach real estate as both an architectural living sanctuary and a strategic financial pillar. My role is not to "sell" you a property, but to uncover asymmetrical opportunities, stress-test pricing assumptions, and ensure complete peace of mind.

Operating across Milan, Lake Como, and prime European residential markets, I partner with discerning private individuals, tech entrepreneurs, and family offices who value efficiency, discretion, and analytical depth.
